Quarterly Business Review Process

The quarterly review combines quantitative analysis with qualitative interpretation.

Review sequence

First, calculate total revenue and total units sold for the requested period.

Second, compare performance by region and product.

Third, review customer satisfaction and churn.

Fourth, identify unusual results and determine which issues require management follow-up.

Interpretation guidelines

Every result should identify the reporting period, metric, and comparison group.

Percentage changes should state the base period.

High revenue does not necessarily indicate high customer satisfaction.

Low customer satisfaction does not establish that a customer will churn.

Escalation guidelines

Escalate unusual results when a metric changes materially compared with a prior period, when a region or product differs substantially from the overall average, or when a customer has both a low satisfaction score and a churn date.

Documentation expectations

A review record should retain the question asked, reporting period, metrics used, comparison performed, and assumptions.

All results from this dataset must be labeled as sample or demonstration results.
